# Catalogue images (stock-photo pass, 2026-10-24)

Before this pass 12 of 12 categories and 37 of 56 products had no image, and 5 product records pointed at files that no longer existed in storage. Real, unbranded stock photographs were chosen by eye (each assignment was reviewed on a labelled contact sheet), re-encoded to 800x800 JPEG, uploaded to the public `product-images` bucket under `catalogue/` with a one-year cache lifetime, and linked through the app's own audit-logged admin RPCs (`admin_update_product` / `admin_update_category`). Nothing is AI-generated and no packaging or brand labels appear in any photo.

Licences: Pexels and Unsplash photos are free for commercial use without attribution. Replace any of them with your own photography at any time from Admin > Products / Categories.

**Approximate photos (marked ~):** for these the stock photo shows the ingredient or type of product, not your exact packet or dish (e.g. a salt photo for the Tata salt lite listings, a noodle dish for Veg hakka). They are placeholders until real packshots exist.
